# Privacy

Lyonoyl Mail keeps your mail, your accounts, and your passwords on your device, and sends nothing about you to the project.

## What reaches the project

- **The check for a newer version**, once a day, only after you tick its box on the first start or turn it on in the settings. It asks the project's site, `lyonoyl.com`, for a small file, naming the product and not its version; like any request, it carries the address your connection comes from.
- **The support page**, when you open it from the settings: your browser asks the site for it.
- **A message to the sponsors' mailbox**, which the maintainers read to answer it.

Everything else Lyonoyl Mail sends goes to your mail provider's servers and, when you ask for them, to the places a message's pictures are kept. None of it reaches the project.

## What the site keeps

The site is static files, with no scripts, no cookies, and no analytics. Netlify serves it, with Cloudflare in front, and each keeps the request logs a web host keeps, under its own privacy policy.
