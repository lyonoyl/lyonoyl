# Code of conduct

Everybody taking part in Lyonoyl Mail—in issues, pull requests, reviews, and anywhere else the project is discussed—keeps the discussion about the work and treats the people in it with respect. It also covers anybody representing the project elsewhere.

## Expected

- Assume good faith, and criticize code and decisions, never people.
- Keep to the subject of the conversation, and accept that a proposal may be declined.
- Protect privacy: post no real message, address, password, or other personal information—your own or anybody else's. A message attached to a report has every real name and address replaced first.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, and discriminatory or sexualized language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, and repeated off-topic posting.
- Publishing anybody's private information without their permission.
- Repeated demands to have a change reviewed, merged, or released.

## Reporting

Report a comment, an issue, or a pull request with GitHub's **Report content**, which sends it to GitHub; conduct by a maintainer is reported the same way. A security flaw is reported as [the security policy](SECURITY.md) says.

## What the maintainers do

The maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content, lock a conversation, and limit or block a participant, for a time or for good, in proportion to what happened.
